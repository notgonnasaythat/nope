How to do it

Set the major & minor axes

Place the drafter and draw the 90 mm major axis. Then turn the drafter 90° and draw the 60 mm minor axis through the centre.

Exam tip: keep construction lines thin and the final curve dark and smooth.

Why this is different

A virtual drawing-board, not a slideshow.

The drafter is animated first, then the construction appears. Students can pause at any point and copy the exact operation onto their own sheet.

  • Movement-based explanation instead of instant finished diagrams
  • Millimetre labels for exam practice
  • Learn Mode explains why; Exam Mode removes distractions
  • Designed for phones as well as laptops

Exam mode

Practice the sequence you will reproduce.

Once a drawing is understood, switch to Exam Mode and follow only the construction order, dimensions and final presentation requirements.

  • Question → Given → Construction steps → Final figure
  • One operation at a time
  • Restart and replay until the motion is familiar
