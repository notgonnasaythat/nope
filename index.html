JNTUH R25 map.

Organized around the five R25 CSE Engineering Drawing and Computer Aided Drafting units.

Unit I — Introduction to Engineering Graphics
Principles, conventions, geometrical constructions, scales, conics and engineering curves.
Unit II — Orthographic Projections
Projection principles, points, lines, regular plane figures, auxiliary planes and CAD orthographic views.
Unit III — Projections of Regular Solids
Prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, sections, sectional views and auxiliary views.
Unit IV — Development of Surfaces
Development of prism, cylinder, pyramid and cone surfaces.
Unit V — Isometric Projections
Isometric scale, simple and compound solids, non-isometric lines, spherical parts and orthographic↔isometric conversion.
