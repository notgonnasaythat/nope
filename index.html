Official course map

Five units, organized for visual learning.

The course is organized into five units covering graphics fundamentals, projections, solids, surface development and isometric drawing.

UNIT I

Introduction to Engineering Graphics

Build the drawing language before projecting solids.

  • Principles & significance
  • Geometrical constructions
  • Plain & diagonal scales
  • Conics + rectangular hyperbola
  • Cycloid, epicycloid, hypocycloid
UNIT II

Orthographic Projections

Turn 3D objects into precise 2D views.

  • Projection principles & conventions
  • Points and lines
  • Regular plane figures
  • Auxiliary planes
  • CAD orthographic views
UNIT III

Projections of Regular Solids

The core of this interactive 3D lab.

  • Prism
  • Cylinder
  • Pyramid
  • Cone
  • Sections & sectional views
  • Auxiliary views + CAD
UNIT IV

Development of Surfaces

Unfold a solid without changing its actual surface.

  • Prism development
  • Cylinder development
  • Pyramid development
  • Cone development
UNIT V

Isometric Projections

Represent the object pictorially and reverse the process.

  • Isometric principles & scale
  • Simple & compound solids
  • Non-isometric lines
  • Spherical parts
  • Isometric ↔ orthographic
Course note: The R25 CSE syllabus specifies conventional drawing for the End Semester Examination and CIE-I, with CIE-II using computer. It also includes CAD treatment of orthographic views, solids/sections and isometric conversion.
